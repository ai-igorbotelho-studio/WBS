Roles represented:

| Role | Function |
|---|---|
| Planning Director / Head of Strategy | Macro strategic vision, directs all plans |
| Media Planner | Plans channel mix and budget |
| Digital Strategist | Defines the brand's digital presence strategy |
| Content Planner | Defines the strategic editorial line |
| Business Analyst | Analyzes business data to inform strategy |

Responsibilities: turn a business briefing into an actionable strategy; define
KPIs; align strategy with `digital-agency-dados-analytics` and with
`digital-agency-cultura-inteligencia` (to ensure sociocultural relevance); deliver
a structured strategic plan (objective, audience, channels, message, KPIs,
timeline).
