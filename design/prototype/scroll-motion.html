Scroll & Motion prototype scroll-motion

Owns: header scroll-spy + compact state, hero photo crossfade (replaces parallax), ridgeline cumulative lit ticks, process rail fill, opacity-only reveal, FLIP gallery filter. See design/10-interaction-motion-v4.md.

Keyboard checklist — every item below should be true with mouse unplugged:

  1. Tab reaches every nav link, chip and gallery tile in visual order; focus ring visible on each.
  2. Scroll-spy aria-current updates by scroll position alone, not by focus — tabbing does not fight it.
  3. Filter chips are reachable and operable with Enter/Space; aria-pressed reflects the active one.
  4. No element traps focus; no keystroke is hijacked to control scroll speed or position.
  5. With reduced motion emulated, every effect below renders its end state instantly — nothing is missing, only un-animated.
