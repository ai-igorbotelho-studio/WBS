01 · Services

Landscape, carpentry and build for the backyard you actually use

Three trades, one team. Every job starts with a site visit and a fixed, itemised quote.

Landscape

Ground work that makes a sloping Waiheke section usable and dry.

  • Timber retaining walls, steps and terraces
  • Paths, drainage and gravel driveways
  • Planting, lawns and garden beds

Carpentry

Outdoor timber work built to handle salt air and winter rain.

  • Decks, pergolas and outdoor rooms
  • Fences, gates and privacy screens
  • Repairs, re-boards and re-stains

Build

Small builds and renovations, from the framing up.

  • Sleepouts, sheds and studio fit-outs
  • Bach renovations and extensions
  • Council and consent guidance

Good questions

How long does a deck or a renovation take?

It depends on scope and site access. Outdoor work is weather- and ferry-dependent, so we build a buffer in.

After the site visit you get an itemised written quote, valid for 30 days. A 30% deposit confirms the booking.

Workmanship is warranted for 12 months from completion.
