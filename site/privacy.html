How we handle your details

This notice explains what Pablo Baldo, trading as Waiheke Backyards Solutions ("we"), does with the personal information you give us, in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect and why

When you send the quote form we collect your name, phone number, email address if you give it, the kind of job and your message. We use them only to reply to you, arrange a site visit and prepare a quote. Giving us your name and phone number is needed to reply; the email address is optional.

Who processes it

The form is delivered by our website host, Netlify, Inc., which stores submissions on our behalf on servers outside New Zealand. From there the details go to our business email and phone. We do not sell or share your information with anyone else, and we do not use analytics, advertising pixels or cookies on this site.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become a job are deleted within [12] months. Details for jobs we do are kept with our quotes, invoices and warranty records for [7] years, as tax and warranty obligations require.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, at any time. Email pablo.wbs@gmail.com or call +64 21 182 2723. If you are not satisfied with our response you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Changes

We will update this notice when our practices change and show the version and date at the top.
