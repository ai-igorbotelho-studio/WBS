06 · FAQ

Good questions

How long does a deck or a renovation take?

It depends on scope and site access. The timeline is set in the quote, before work starts, and outdoor work is weather- and ferry-dependent, so we build a buffer in and tell you as soon as anything moves.

How does the quote work?

After the site visit you get an itemised written quote, valid for 30 days. A 30% deposit confirms the booking. The balance is invoiced on completion, due within 7 days; for jobs over $10,000 we agree progress payments in writing.

How do I know what is happening on site?

Progress photos and a short update on WhatsApp on every working day, plus a walk-through with Pablo at each milestone.

Is the work guaranteed?

Workmanship is warranted for 12 months from completion. Manufacturer warranties on supplied materials are passed through to you.

Where do you work?

Anywhere on Waiheke Island. We are based on the island, so there is no ferry surcharge for the crew.
