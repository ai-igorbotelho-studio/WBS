Terms & Conditions

These terms govern the supply of landscape, carpentry and construction services by Pablo Baldo, trading as Waiheke Backyards Solutions, to clients on Waiheke Island, New Zealand.

  1. Quotes & estimates

    All quotes are valid for 30 days from the date of issue. Quotes are based on the scope and site information available at the time. Variations to scope, materials, or site conditions may result in adjustments to the price, communicated in writing before work proceeds.

  2. Deposits & payment

    A deposit of 30% of the quoted total is required to confirm the booking and reserve materials. The remaining balance is invoiced on completion, with payment due within 7 days. For projects exceeding $10,000, progress payments may be agreed in writing.

  3. Materials & sourcing

    Materials are sourced locally where practical. Substitutions of equivalent quality may be made where supply is delayed. Any client-supplied materials remain the client's responsibility for sourcing, condition, and timely delivery.

  4. Site access & conditions

    The client is responsible for providing safe, lawful and timely access to the site, including driveway/entry permissions, water and power where required. Delays caused by inaccessible sites may be charged at a standard call-out rate.

  5. Weather & timing

    Outdoor work is weather-dependent. Reasonable efforts will be made to keep to agreed timelines; rescheduling due to weather, ferry disruption, or supplier delays is not grounds for cancellation.

  6. Variations & additional work

    Any work outside the original scope will be quoted separately and confirmed in writing before commencing. Verbal requests will be confirmed by message or email to avoid misunderstandings.

  7. Warranty

    Workmanship is warranted for 12 months from the date of completion. The warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, neglect, weather events, or modifications by other parties. Manufacturer warranties on supplied materials are passed through to the client.

  8. Cancellation

    Cancellations made more than 7 days before the scheduled start are refundable less any non-recoverable material costs. Cancellations within 7 days of start may forfeit the deposit.

  9. Liability

    Waiheke Backyards Solutions carries general liability insurance. Liability is limited to the value of the contracted work. The client is responsible for insuring contents and structures during and after works.

  10. Disputes

    Any disputes will first be addressed directly between the parties in good faith. Unresolved matters will be settled under the laws of New Zealand.
